# Chinese character stroke data

The lightweight `character_catalog.txt` index is generated from
[Make Me a Hanzi](https://github.com/skishore/makemeahanzi). Stroke data is
downloaded on demand from the `hanzi-writer-data` distribution and then cached
locally for offline use.

The graphical data is derived from Arphic PL KaitiM GB and Arphic PL UKai and
is redistributed under the Arphic Public License. The applicable upstream
notices are stored in `tools/vendor/makemeahanzi/`.

The Kid Learning project does not redraw or AI-generate strokes. It preserves
the upstream stroke order, stroke outlines, and median points.
